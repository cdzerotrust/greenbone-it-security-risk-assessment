# Risk Management Workbook

This directory contains the supporting risk management workbook
developed for the Greenbone IT Security Risk Assessment.

The workbook includes four worksheets:

1. Risk Register
2. Risk Scoring Methodology
3. Risk Treatment Plan
4. Executive Dashboard

The workbook documents 12 distinct risks identified from the
detailed review of 13 Critical Greenbone findings.

Nine risks were classified as Critical and three as High.

All risk ratings, ownership assignments, and treatment
recommendations are based on a simulated production scenario.

No remediation or residual risk assessment was completed.
